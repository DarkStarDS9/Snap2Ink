# Privacy Policy — Snap2Ink

_Last updated: 19 August 2026_

**Snap2Ink collects nothing, sends nothing to us, and has no servers.** It is an offline app: the
only thing it ever talks to is your own e-ink display, over Bluetooth, in the same room.

## What the app touches, and where it goes

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Photos you take in the app | To dither them and show them on your display | Processed entirely on your iPhone, then sent over Bluetooth to your own display. Nothing leaves your phone by any other route. |
| Your photo library (optional) | If you turn on backup, prints are saved to a "Snap2Ink" album so you can resend one later | Stays on your device and in your own iCloud Photos, if you use it. Snap2Ink only adds to and reads back its own album. |
| App settings (dither algorithm, backup on/off) | To remember your choices | Stored locally on the device. |
| Bluetooth | To find and connect to your display | Local radio only. No location is derived from it, and Snap2Ink does not request location access. |

## What Snap2Ink does not do

- No accounts, no login, no email address.
- **No network requests at all.** The app contains no HTTP client and contacts no web service —
  not ours, not anyone's.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party
  SDKs of any kind.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregate, anonymous download and crash statistics through
App Store Connect. That is Apple's data collection, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and it
does not identify you.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** — to take the photo you print. Required for the app's core function.
- **Photos (add / read)** — optional, only if you enable print backup.
- **Bluetooth** — to connect to your display.

Denying any of these leaves the rest of the app working; Snap2Ink never asks for a permission it
isn't about to use.

## Children

Snap2Ink is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.
